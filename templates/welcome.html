    {# 6. A quick tour: how to use the app (swipe) #}

    How it works

    Plan a walk in seconds

    On Walk, ask for what you're in the mood for, or tap Draw area and circle a part of the map. The AI picks the stops, and Start gives you turn-by-turn directions.

    Your city's feed

    Stories shows what's good in your city: stories, Walks this week and walks other walkers shared. Tap a walk to preview it, or search any city at the top to see its feed.

    Save, build and share

    Save places you love to My places (the tab on the right of the map) and build your own routes: a date night, a coffee loop, a tour for friends. Share sends a link; Post to feed shows it to walkers in your city.

    Rate with footprints

    When you reach a stop, give it 1 to 5 footprints: how worth the walk it was. Your ratings help everyone, and the AI plans better walks from them.
